## Teaching Across Levels

One of the things I value about teaching is the opportunity to work with students at very different stages of their academic development. I have taught introductory undergraduates encountering political science for the first time, master's and doctoral students developing quantitative research skills, and researchers seeking specialized training in Bayesian methods.

Across these settings, my aim is to make complex ideas understandable without stripping away the reasoning that makes them useful.
